## Brand & Style
This design system curates a serene, eye-friendly, luxury medical-grade experience engineered for bedside nocturnal respiratory intelligence. Blending the quiet authority of high-end Swiss horology and bespoke medical instrumentation, it reconciles clinical rigor with restorative nocturnal ergonomics. 

The aesthetic merges **Glassmorphism** with **Minimalism**: velvety deep sapphire-slate and obsidian noir tiers, delicate micro-borders, brushed champagne-gold accents, and bioluminescent celadon-cyan signal paths. Every interaction is soft, glare-mitigated, and intentional—delivering critical clinical telemetry without disturbing sleep architecture or inducing sensory fatigue.

## Colors
The color architecture resolves the challenge of overnight bedside monitoring: zero light bleed, zero glare, and high chromatic fidelity. The palette avoids pitch-black dead zones and harsh stark whites, leaning instead into deep sapphire-slate foundations layered with radiant, low-luminance accents.

- **Primary (`#D4AF37` — Champagne Gold):** Denotes precision calibration, system status, active focus states, and luxury device touchpoints. Balanced by `#E2C974` for interactive highlights and `#C5A059` for subtle borders.
- **Secondary (`#2DD4BF` / `#4ECCA3` — Celadon Mint):** Represents physiological homeostasis, uncompromised airway dynamics, regular respiratory cycles, and acoustic health stability.
- **Tertiary (`#38BDF8` — Frosted Cyan):** Reserved for active acoustic telemetry, frequency spectrum analysis, micro-phonetic audio waveforms, and AI algorithmic inference.
- **Alert Rose (`#FF6B81`):** Calibrated for high-contrast visibility under nocturnal dark-adaptation, avoiding jarring scarlet flashes while providing unambiguous triage alerts for apneas and paroxysms.
- **Surface Foundations:**
  - `obsidian-base (#090D16)`: Viewport floor and outer chassis.
  - `midnight-slate (#0D1424)`: Primary baseline canvas for telemetry views.
  - `sapphire-surface (#131D31)`: Standard card and module surface.
  - `sapphire-elevated (#18233C)`: Popovers, floating trays, and active sensor states.

## Typography
Typographic choices prioritize split-second comprehension in low-lux conditions:

- **Plus Jakarta Sans:** Selected for section headers, diagnostic module titles, and high-level patient overviews. Its geometry provides modern elegance with warmth.
- **Inter:** Drives analytical summaries, medical explanations, and triage instructions. Optimized for optical micro-clarity across high-DPI glass screens.
- **JetBrains Mono:** Dedicated exclusively to clinical data points, numeric metrics, timestamping, and vital telemetry (e.g., Breaths Per Minute, decibel noise floors, SPO2 baselines). Tabular numerical alignment guarantees numbers remain rock-steady during high-frequency live acoustic streaming.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a fluid-hybrid architecture structured on an 8px spatial interval. The visual environment demands airy breathing room around high-density data channels to keep cognitive tension low.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout with 20px gutters (`gutter-desktop`) and 32px screen margins. Continuous spectrogram streams run horizontally across full or 8-column bands, anchored by persistent 4-column diagnostic cards.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with 16px gutters, dual-pane waveform visualization, and touch-optimized controls calibrated for two-hand bedside holding.
- **Mobile (360px - 767px):** 4-column layout with 12px gutters (`gutter-mobile`). Telemetry graphs collapse into vertically stacked rhythm monitors; critical apnea/respiratory alarms dock to safe bottom viewport thumb zones.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is articulated through velvety tonal stratification, high-precision ghost rims, and subtle bioluminescent glows rather than opaque drop shadows:

- **Atmospheric Ground (Level 0):** Deepest background `#090D16` overlaid with a subtle radial gradient radiating from active sensor positions.
- **Velvet Wells (Level 1):** `#0D1424` recessed containers with `inset 0 1px 2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)` dedicated to acoustic waveform viewports.
- **Card Chassis (Level 2):** `#131D31` featuring glassmorphic backdrop filters (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), bounded by micro-borders: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)`.
- **Luxe Accent Trays (Level 3):** `#18233C` overlaid with subtle champagne-gold rim lighting: `1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.22)` accompanied by diffused ambient lighting: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08)`.
- **Telemetry Glow Signatures:**
  - Stable vitals project a tranquil celadon-mint aura (`box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(46, 212, 191, 0.15)`).
  - Acoustic spectral channels cast a faint cyan ambient back-glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.12)`).

## Shapes
The shape system balances modern ergonomic softness with technical precision (`roundedness: 2`). Standard modules and diagnostic cards feature an 8px to 16px corner radius (`rounded-lg` to `rounded-xl`), creating comfortable, organic boundaries. 

Pill shapes are reserved strictly for operational mode toggles, status pills, and timecode scrubs, while inner telemetry data blocks retain disciplined 4px to 8px radii to sustain medical device structure.

## Components

### Telemetry & Acoustic Waveform Cards
- **Construction:** Velvety glassmorphism with `#131D31` fill, `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)` micro-borders, and `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`.
- **Top Bar:** Houses module nomenclature in `label-caps` (`JetBrains Mono`, 10px, Slate-400), real-time millisecond signal quality indicators, and champagne-gold action icons.
- **Plot Area:** Embedded in recessed `#0D1424` panels. Real-time acoustic wave polylines are rendered in Frosted Cyan (`#38BDF8`) or Celadon Mint (`#2DD4BF`), illuminated by soft SVG drop-glow filters.

### Buttons & Action Controls
- **Primary Luxe Action:** Satin finish champagne gold (`#D4AF37`), dark midnight foreground (`#090D16`), weight 600. Features a delicate outer gold wash on hover (`0 0 16px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.3)`) and subtle tactile compression (`scale: 0.98`) on press.
- **Secondary Ghost Action:** Translucent dark sapphire (`rgba(19, 29, 49, 0.7)`), 1px border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`), typography in Slate-100. Hover transitions the border to subtle gold (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.4)`).
- **Bedside Mute / Dim Control:** Low-luminance Obsidian toggle with a glowing indicator lamp that throttles viewport brightness down to 5% baseline.

### Telemetry Badges & Status Chips
- **Normative State:** Pill form factor, `background: rgba(45, 212, 191, 0.1)`, `border: 1px solid rgba(45, 212, 191, 0.3)`, text `#2DD4BF`. Prefixed by a pulsing 6px celadon beacon dot.
- **Observation State:** `background: rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.1)`, `border: 1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.3)`, text `#E2C974`.
- **Critical Alert State:** `background: rgba(255, 107, 129, 0.12)`, `border: 1px solid rgba(255, 107, 129, 0.4)`, text `#FF6B81`.

### Numeric Readouts & Indicators
- **Metric Groups:** Large tabular figures (`telemetry-hero` or `telemetry-val`) stacked over uppercase subheaders in `label-caps`. Unit markers (e.g., `bpm`, `dB(A)`, `cmH2O`) are scaled to 50% font size, colored in muted slate, and baseline-aligned to preserve optical stability.

### Input Fields & Sensitivity Dials
- **Text Inputs:** Recessed `#0D1424` background with ghost border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. Focus states activate a crisp gold rim (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px #D4AF37`) without jarring bright flashes.
- **Bedside Sliders:** 3px background track in `#18233C`, active fill in gradient champagne-gold, and an illuminated 14px circular thumb with a soft ambient cyan halo.
